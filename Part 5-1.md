# Epilogue

The island breathes again. The adventurers stand amidst thawing earth as waterfalls cascade from once-frozen cliffs. Seabirds circle overhead, filling the skies with sound and color long absent from Raijinsho. The Glowing Gate’s crew cheer wildly, singing shanties of treasure and victory.

The human Vaelithra—still disguised as the newspaper girl—approaches the party. Her small hands clutch the leather notebook she always carries, her eyes shimmering with pride and something deeper, older.

She smiles, voice soft but certain:

> “The shard is safe again. One out of ten—”

She freezes mid-sentence, her eyes widening ever so slightly. Then she forces a laugh, correcting herself quickly:

> “Eleven. One out of eleven remain. Forgive me. Sometimes… I lose count.”

An odd stillness follows, like the sea itself listening. If pressed, she will dodge with a childlike grin, but her mask falters—just for a moment.

As she walks toward the horizon, she adds quietly, almost to herself:

> “When the last is found, I will finally… comeback.”

The adventurers are left with gold, glory, and questions that echo louder than any dragon’s roar.

The sails of the Glowing Gate rise once more, catching a warm, welcoming wind. But the players now know their journey has only begun.


This one-shot is part of a larger narrative within the Malvidra Twisted World universe. 
The time took place on Year 35 A.S (After Sundering), on  the 6th Sol'thara, Sylvryn Day.
A pirate crew in need of help to raid a treasure guarded island by an Adult White Dragon named, Harann Nemmonis, which already made the Island of Raijinsho her lair, no matter the weather, the island is covered in ice and snow. the White Dragon is residing inside her cave in the middle of the island, there are some followers of the dragon that reside all around the island and guards the cave as no one dares to disturb the dragon. Unknowingly, out of all the treasure, there's a pond inside the cave which a place the dragon never know and never seems to care, inside the pond lives a Koi Fish, it's scale is silvery and bright, it's a Vaelithra, one of Vaeil, the God of Time and Keeper of the Archive's fragment souls. The Koi Vaelithra reside there first long before the dragon come, and by it's magic the pond stays unfrozen and warm, unaffected by the dragon's magic. Our adventure begins at the Thundrum Port, many ships of pirates, fisherman or even just a visitors dock their ships there. One of the ship, Glowing Gate, is belong to a pirate crew of Captain Thania Schmidt, her crew aim to raid the Island of Raijinsho and intend to claim the treasure that lays in the lair of Harann Nemmosis, the White Dragon. but Thania's not stupid, she knew her crew's not strong enough, so she hire a newspaper girl on the port, to deliver the message to all the people there that her crew needed more power. Our adventurers would encounter with this newspaper girl which is also a Vaelithra but in human form, she's happily passed the message to our adventurers and persuade them to help, when in truth this Vaelithra needed the adventurers to meet the Vaelithra that was residing in Raijinsho.

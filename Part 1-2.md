# Running the Adventure

This section is intended only for the Dungeon Master (DM). It contains the core framework, necessary resources, and the full background for the adventure.

## Overview

***Vaelithra - Into Raijinsho*** is a high-level one-shot designed for a party of four to six characters of **Level 9**. The adventure combines environmental survival, tense negotiation, and a climactic confrontation with a formidable Adult White Dragon, all while advancing a secret, divine meta-plot.

* **Author/Universe:** Malvidra Twisted World
* **World:** Dewanegara
* **Focus:** **Survival** (Extreme Cold), **Exploration** (Labyrinthine Lair), and **High-Level Combat**.
* **Timeline:** **6th Sol'thara, Sylvryn Day, Year 35 A.S. (After Sundering)**.

### Required Materials

To run this adventure, the DM will need access to the core Fifth Edition rulebooks for rules on extreme environments and general mechanics. **Note:** Custom stat blocks will be required for the corrupted minions and specialized dragon-kin.

| Resource Book | Key Content to Prepare | Purpose for this Adventure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Player's Handbook (PHB)** | **Core Rules, Spells, Skill Checks** | Essential for all player abilities and rules mechanics. |
| **Dungeon Master's Guide (DMG)** | **Extreme Cold (p. 110), Creating Monster Stat Blocks, Treasure** | Crucial for tracking the perpetual winter and balancing the homebrewed enemy challenges. |

## Adventure Background

### The Sundering War and Vaeil's Intervention

The world of **Dewanegara** was once ravaged by the **Sundering War**, a prolonged conflict fought between the Gods and the Demons. **Vaeil**, the God of Time and Keeper of the Archives, stood apart, observing the destructive stalemate. Concerned that the war would end in a mutual catastrophe, Vaeil intervened, subtly manipulating the currents of time to create a decisive outcome: the Gods emerged as the victors, while the Demons were spared total annihilation.

This manipulation, however, was known to powerful entities on both sides. The Gods, intent on preserving Vaeil’s *rewritten Archives* to secure their victory, and the Demons, intent on finding and controlling the Archives to seize power, both sought Vaeil's domain. To protect his work and the balance he created, **Vaeil shattered his own soul into twelve fragments, known as the Vaelithra**, scattering them across Dewanegara and sometimes even beyond time.

### The Vaelithra Fragments

These twelve **Vaelithra** fragments have manifested in diverse forms—as items, locations, living beings, or even spirits attached to a host. They are the keys to Vaeil's eventual resurrection and the final struggle for the Archives.

* **The Koi Vaelithra (The Second Fragment):** This fragment has taken the form of a silvery **Koi Fish** and resides in an ancient, enchanted pond deep within **Raijinsho Island**. Its magic is so old it keeps the pond warm and entirely unaffected by the dragon's overwhelming cold.
* **The Human Vaelithra (The Guiding Fragment):** The **Newspaper Girl** who approaches the party is a pivotal Vaelithra entity. She is tasked with appearing in sequential adventures to guide heroes to the locations of the other eleven fragments, paving the way for the larger, long-term campaign about Vaeil's resurrection.

### Harann's Reign of Terror

**Harann Nemmonis**, the vicious **Adult White Dragon**, has claimed Raijinsho Island as her territory. Her magical control over the environment is absolute, locking the island in perpetual, lethal winter.

* **The Dragon's Army:** Harann's power corrupts the very corpses of her foes. Raiders who failed to kill her have been twisted into **Corrupted Ice Zombies**—mindless automatons that patrol the exterior. Her skilled forces consist of organized, fanatical **Dragon-Kin Soldiers** who guard the labyrinthine cave complex.

## Adventure Outline

The party is hired under the false pretense of gold by **Captain Thania Schmidt**, who seeks to exploit the adventurers’ power. They are, in fact, answering a divine summons to retrieve the Koi Vaelithra.

| Chapter | Title | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chapter 1** | The Hook at Thundrum Port | Social Interaction and Roleplaying (Act 0). Negotiation, lore introduction, and the subtle influence of the Human Vaelithra. |
| **Chapter 2** | The Journey and the Cold | Environmental Challenge. The 48-hour sea voyage, testing the party with **Extreme Cold** rules and high tension with the pirate crew. |
| **Chapter 3** | Into the Labyrinth of Ice | Exploration and Combat. Navigating the frozen sea, battling the corrupted minions, exploring the icy caves, and the climactic encounter with **Harann Nemmonis** and the discovery of the Koi Vaelithra. |

### Getting Started

The adventure begins with the party established as capable adventurers, perhaps resting or resupplying, at the docks of **Thundrum Port** on the **6th Sol'thara, Sylvryn Day**.

The DM should begin the session by reading or paraphrasing the opening description for **Chapter 1: The Hook at Thundrum Port**.

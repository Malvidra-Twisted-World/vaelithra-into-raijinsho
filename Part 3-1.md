# Chapter 2: The Frozen Lair

## Setting the Scene

As the adventurers step ashore, the air grows unnaturally frigid. Every breath hangs in the air like smoke. The cove leads into a dense pine forest, each tree encased in frost. The snow crunches beneath boots, and faint howls echo across the island—though no wolves should live here.

The path winds upward toward jagged cliffs. At its heart lies **Harann Nemmonis**’s lair: a yawning cave half-hidden in ice. But the dragon is not alone. Her corruption has twisted both men and beasts, cursing them into grotesque guardians of the island. The air here tastes like broken glass.

### Key Scenes and Encounters

#### The Icy Approach (Exploration + Ambush)

The party follows a frozen trail littered with broken weapons and skeletal remains.

* **Survival (DC 13):** Notice claw marks not from wolves, but from something reptilian.
* **Investigation (DC 15):** Discover frozen bodies of sailors, their faces warped mid-scream. One sailor's hand, frozen in rigor mortis, clutches a **logbook**. The book's pages are brittle and coated in a thin layer of ice, but with a successful **Arcana check (DC 14)** or by melting the ice, the players can read the final entry: *"The ship is lost. The frost whispers her name. Harann… It's not the gold she values, but the warmth..."*

##### Combat Encounter (Ambush):
A band of **Frost-Cursed Kobolds** leaps from the treeline—once humans, now shriveled by dragon-curse into kobold-like creatures with cracked, icy scales.

* 1 **Frost-Cursed Chieftain** (reskinned Kobold Inventor, CR 1, but with cold damage abilities)
* 6–8 **Frost-Cursed Kobolds** (reskinned Kobolds with Pack Tactics, +1d4 cold damage on hit)

**Tactics:**

* They ambush with crude icy javelins.
* When one dies, its body explodes in a burst of icy shards (**DC 12 Dex save** or **1d6 cold damage**).

**Foreshadowing:** These kobolds hiss prayers not to Tiamat, but to “The White Lady of Frost.”

#### The Frozen Caverns (Dungeon Exploration)

The trail leads into caverns carved from ice and stone. The dungeon has three key areas, filled with a silence so deep it feels like pressure in the ears. The ground is treacherously slippery; a creature moving more than half its speed must make a **DC 10 Acrobatics check** or fall prone.

##### Area A: The Crystal Pond

A small chamber where a pond remains unfrozen, glowing faintly.

* Players who approach see koi-like shadows swimming beneath the surface.
* **Arcana DC 15:** The pond is sustained by powerful time-magic. (This foreshadows the Vaelithra.)

##### Area B: The Bone Gallery

The walls are lined with ice-encased corpses—former sailors, hunters, even pirates who braved Raijinsho.

* **Perception DC 14:** One body twitches—the dragon’s curse tries to reanimate them.
* **Combat Trigger:** 3 Frost-Cursed Mariners (undead bandits with icy claws) break free.

##### Area C: The Antechamber of Frost

A vast chamber before the lair proper. The air bites with unnatural cold (all creatures make a **DC 12 Con save** every **10 minutes** or gain 1 level of exhaustion). From above, icy stalactites hang menacingly; any loud noise or thunder spell will cause one to fall (**DC 15 Dex save** or take **2d6 bludgeoning damage**).

* 2 **Frost-Cursed Ogres** (reskinned Ogres with cold damage resistance and icy clubs).
* The chamber contains piles of half-frozen treasure—bait to lure the greedy.

### Vaelithra’s Whisper

Before the adventurers step into the dragon’s lair, the disguised Vaelithra slips away from the crew and appears in the chamber. She places her hand against the frozen wall, whispering:

*“This cave hides more than gold. Do not forget—the White Dragon does not own what lies beneath the pond. It is older than her, older than all of us.”*

* **Insight DC 16:** She’s not warning them out of fear of the dragon, but out of reverence for what lives within.

### Pacing Options

* If you want this chapter shorter: run only Area A (Pond) → Area C (Ogres) → Dragon’s Lair.
* If you want full dungeon crawl: include all areas with additional traps (icy floors, collapsing tunnels, frozen illusions).

At the end of Chapter 2, the adventurers finally step into the dragon’s lair—vast, glittering with frozen hoards of treasure, the sound of dripping water echoing like a heartbeat. Somewhere deeper, Harann Nemmonis stirs.

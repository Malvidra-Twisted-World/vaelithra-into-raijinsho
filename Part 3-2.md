# Chapter 2: The Journey and the Cold

This chapter covers the two-day, high-tension voyage to **Raijinsho Island**. It is designed to drain some of the party's resources (spells, hit dice) through environmental challenges and establish the mistrust between the adventurers and the pirate crew.

## Setting Sail

The adventure immediately shifts from negotiation to preparation.

> [!info] **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The docks of **Thundrum Port** fall away behind you as the **Glowing Gate** is quickly maneuvered out into the open water. The ship is sturdy, but loud—every timber groans as the vessel catches the wind. Captain Thania gives you a single, grim nod as the sun begins its slow descent. You have approximately **forty-eight hours** of travel ahead. With every league sailed, the air grows colder, and the sea becomes darker and more turbulent, building a palpable sense of dread as you head toward the magically frozen silhouette of **Raijinsho Island**.

### DM Information
The journey takes 48 hours. The focus here should be on the relentless, creeping danger of the environment.

## The Chill of Nemmonis

As the *Glowing Gate* nears the island, it enters the **Regional Effects** of the Adult White Dragon, **Harann Nemmonis**. The cold is unnaturally intense and punishing.

* **The First Day (The Watch):** During the first 24 hours of the voyage, the air temperature drops drastically. All characters are considered to be in an area of **Cold Weather**. Characters who are not wearing **cold weather gear** or protected by magic suffer disadvantage on ability checks that rely on Dexterity.
* **The Second Day (Extreme Cold):** After the first 24 hours, the crew is sailing in an area of **Extreme Cold**.
    * **DM Rule:** Refer to the **Dungeon Master's Guide (DMG)**, *Chapter 5: Adventure Environments* for the rules on **Extreme Cold (p. 110)**. Characters must succeed on a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw** at the end of each hour or gain one level of **Exhaustion**. Those wearing cold weather gear or protected by magic automatically succeed. This resource drain is meant to make the initial landing on Raijinsho feel earned and desperate.


## First Contact (Foreshadowing Skirmish)

On the second night, roughly 36 hours into the voyage, the *Glowing Gate* drifts close enough to the **frozen sea** to be noticed by the dragon's corrupted minions. This encounter provides the party with their first taste of Harann's army.

> **Read-Aloud Text:**
> You are deep into the second night. The ship is encased in a crust of frost, and the constant moan of the wind is broken by the creak of the hull fighting the waves. Suddenly, the lookout shouts a warning. In the gloom, figures are pulling themselves out of the frigid water onto the ship's railing—their skin is the color of old ice, and their movements are stiff, unnatural, driven by something other than life.

### DM Information
Run this as a brief, desperate encounter.

* **Enemies:** Deploy **two to three** of your homebrewed **Corrupted Ice Zombies** (use modified **Zombie** or **Ghoul** stat blocks, granting them resistance to cold damage and a slowing effect on hits, like the *chill touch* spell).
* **Objective:** This is not a major combat, but a quick defense. The **Corrupted Ice Zombies** aim to kill or disable the closest living crew member, proving that the dragon's influence extends far out to sea.
* **Tension:** Captain Thania (or her crew) will shout for the adventurers to handle the threat, focusing on protecting the ship's rigging and helm rather than engaging directly.

#### Development
The fight should be over quickly. Once the corrupted minions are defeated (and they do not surrender), their icy bodies shatter and dissolve back into the frigid sea.

* **Lore Drop:** Captain Thania approaches afterward, clearly shaken. "Those are the damned fools who failed to raid her five years ago. Harann uses their corpses to guard her borders. Kill them quickly, or they'll turn you into one of them." This confirms the fate of failed raiders and reinforces the terrifying scope of the dragon's power.

## The Crew's Grudges

The party is traveling with pirates who resent sharing the hard-won treasure with mercenaries. This provides roleplaying opportunities and tension.

**First Mate Kaelen** (use **Bandit** statistics with advantage on Intimidation checks) is the most vocal opponent.

* **Roleplaying Note:** Kaelen is openly contemptuous. She believes the party will doom the ship and should not receive the share of magic items.
* **Confrontation:** At some point during the journey, Kaelen confronts the party, likely at dinner or during a cold shift on deck. She accuses them of cowardice or greed.
    * **Options:** The party can respond with **Charisma (Intimidation)** or **Charisma (Persuasion)** (both **DC 15**) to shut her down or ease tensions. A failure results in Kaelen sulking and refusing to share information. A success sees Kaelen grudgingly respect the adventurers and share a final, key piece of local knowledge: "The Dragon-Kin Soldiers carry horns. If they blow them, the ice *moves*."

### Development

The 48 hours conclude as the *Glowing Gate* is forced to halt its progress. The water is no longer liquid, but a jagged, impossibly wide sheet of ancient, frozen sea.

The characters are now ready to disembark and face the final chapter.

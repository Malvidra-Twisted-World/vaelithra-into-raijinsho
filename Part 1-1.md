# Introduction

This one-shot adventure is part of the ongoing **Malvidra Twisted World's: Vaelithra** saga. It takes place in the year **35 A.S. (After Sundering)**, on the **6th of Sol’thara, Sylvryn Day**, when the seas of Dewanegara churn with legends of dragons, pirates, and forgotten fragments of divinity.

Far to the east lies the **Island of Raijinsho**, an isle perpetually locked in winter’s grasp. Ice coats its cliffs, snow buries its jungles, and storms gather endlessly around it. This unnatural frost is not born of nature, but of the adult white dragon **Harann Nemmonis**, who has claimed the island as her lair.

Beneath the dragon’s frozen hoard lies a secret even Harann ignores: a **silver-scaled koi** gliding in the warmth of a hidden pond. This koi is no ordinary creature, but a fragment of the god **Vaeil, Keeper of the Archive and Master of Time**—known as **Vaelithra**. Protected by subtle divine magic, the pond remains unfrozen despite the dragon’s presence.

Our story begins not on Raijinsho, but at **Thundrum Port**, a bustling harbor where pirate ships, merchant vessels, and fisherfolk all vie for space. Among the crowd lies the **Glowing Gate**, a pirate ship under the command of **Captain Thania Schmidt**, a daring raider who seeks Raijinsho’s treasures. Knowing her crew alone is not enough to challenge a dragon, Thania spreads word in the port, hiring new blood.

It is here the adventurers will encounter **Vaelithra in human guise**, a spirited newspaper girl with eager eyes. With her voice and flyers, she spreads Thania’s message across the port—yet beneath her playful charm lies a deeper truth: she seeks not treasure, but to bring the heroes face-to-face with her divine fragment self hidden on Raijinsho.

## Running This Adventure

This adventure is designed for characters of **levels 9**.
At this tier, adventurers have powerful abilities but are still mortal enough that a dragon remains a terrifying challenge.

The adventure combines:

* **Roleplay** at Thundrum Port (meeting Vaelithra and Captain Thania).
* **Combat** against the dragon’s followers and the white dragon herself.
* **Lore reveal** of Vaelithra, fragment of Vaeil, through divine time-shifting events.

### Themes

* **Pirates and peril.** Adventurers deal with shady bargains, risky allies, and the promise of dragon treasure.
* **Frozen dread.** The unnatural winter of Raijinsho creates an alien, hostile environment.
* **Time’s intervention.** Vaelithra’s powers may shift time itself, creating narrative “rewinds” when the heroes fall.

The one-shot should take about **4–6 hours** to play, depending on the group’s pace.

## Adventure Hooks

Here are some ways you can draw your players into the story:

* **The Promise of Treasure.** A fortune lies in **Raijinsho**, and word has spread quickly. Gold, artifacts, and forgotten relics await.
* **Pirate’s Call.** Captain Thania Schmidt is looking for brave souls at **Thundrum Port**—any adventurer with a taste for danger can be tempted.
* **Rumors of the Divine.** Scholars and clerics whisper of a strange “**warm pond**” on **Raijinsho** where magic flows against the dragon’s frost. Such rumors might intrigue anyone tied to divine or arcane power.
* **The Newspaper Girl.** The human form of **Vaelithra** herself approaches the adventurers, persuading them with childlike charm and promise of fame if they succeed.

## The Port of Thundrum

### Scene: Thundrum Port

The smell of brine, tar, and sizzling street food fills the air. Sailors curse as crates swing overhead, merchants bark prices, and gulls screech above the crowded harbor. Amidst the chaos, a high, cheerful voice cuts through:

> “Extra! Extra! Crew wanted for the *Glowing Gate*! Glory, riches, and the dragon’s hoard await!”

A small figure darts between dockworkers, waving a stack of papers. It’s a **young girl in patched clothes**, her eyes alight with mischief and curiosity. She thrusts one of the flyers toward the adventurers.

#### Vaelithra’s Approach (in human form)

**Roleplay Notes:**

- She will introduce herself as **Renata**
* She acts excitable, like a child selling papers, but slips in oddly wise phrases now and then.
* Her main goal: get the adventurers intrigued and lead them to Captain Thania.
* She never lies, but often avoids direct answers about herself.

**Example Dialogue:**

* *“You look strong! Stronger than the usual drunkards around here. Raijinsho needs heroes, don’t you think?”*
* *“They say Harann Nemmonis sleeps on a mountain of gold. I say, what’s a little frostbite if it means fortune?”*
* If asked why she’s helping: *“Oh, I just… like stories. And you, I think, could make a great one.”*

**Skill Checks:**

* **Insight DC 16:** She’s hiding something; her excitement feels rehearsed, as though she’s nudging fate itself.
* **Arcana or Religion DC 18:** Her presence has a faint divine aura, though well-hidden.

#### Enter Captain Thania Schmidt

Moments later, a ripple passes through the crowd as **Captain Thania** arrives. A tall woman with sun-bleached hair and a scar along her jaw, she commands attention with a swagger and a predator’s smile. Her coat is crimson, lined with gold, and a **cutlass hangs at her hip**—its hilt polished from use.

She regards the adventurers like goods on display.

**Example Dialogue:**

* *“So, you’re the ones my little herald dragged in? Good. I’ve no patience for cowards.”*
* *“We’re bound for Raijinsho. An island locked in ice, where Harann Nemmonis guards her hoard. I won’t sugarcoat it: it’s a deathtrap. But with you on my side… well, perhaps we’ll beat the odds.”*
* *“Help me, and you’ll have your share. Refuse, and I’ll find someone else—though not as promising, I’m sure.”*

**Skill Checks:**

* **Persuasion DC 15:** Secure a better cut of the treasure (one extra magic item roll or +10% gold).
* **Intimidation DC 15:** Make Thania respect their strength—she won’t admit it, but she’ll hold back less information later.
* **Insight DC 14:** Thania is hiding her desperation; she knows her crew alone doesn’t stand a chance.

#### The Choice

The scene should end with the adventurers deciding whether to:

1. **Join Thania’s raid** (the intended path).
2. **Probe Vaelithra further** (risk revealing her secret early).
3. **Refuse** (Vaelithra will press harder, subtly nudging them—time “feels” off if they decline).

**Narrative Hook if They Hesitate:**
The newspaper girl tilts her head, almost knowingly, and whispers:

> *“You don’t want to miss this story. It’s… important.”*
> Her eyes glimmer silver for the briefest second before returning to normal.

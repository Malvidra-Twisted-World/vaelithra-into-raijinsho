# Introduction

## Running the Adventure

This adventure is intended for the **Dungeon Master (DM)** and assumes familiarity with the **Fifth Edition** core rules. The following section introduces the tone, world context, and framework for running ***Vaelithra – Into Raijinsho*** within the **Malvidra Twisted World** setting.

This module emphasizes **exploration and survival** rather than extended combat — each encounter is deliberate, deadly, and meant to be memorable. The focus lies on the island’s oppressive cold, the eerie stillness of a forgotten people, and the divine mystery lurking beneath the ice.

---

## Overview

***Vaelithra – Into Raijinsho*** is a high-level one-shot designed for **Level 7–9** characters, though it is best experienced at **Level 9** for full narrative and mechanical tension. It serves as both a standalone expedition and a key part of the ongoing **Vaelithra Saga**, where fragments of the shattered god **Vaeil** appear across time and space.

* **Author / Universe:** Malvidra Twisted World
* **World:** Dewanegara
* **Primary Themes:** *Exploration (Frozen Island), Survival (Extreme Cold), Divine Mystery*
* **Tone:** Harsh isolation, hidden divinity, and brief warmth in the dark
* **Timeline:** 6th Sol’thara, Sylvryn Day, Year 35 A.S. (After Sundering)

---

## Adventure Summary

The adventurers are drawn into the service of **Captain Thania Schmidt**, a hardened pirate chasing an old legend of the **Sacred Eye**—a brotherhood of thieves who once ruled the seas around Thundrum City. Her goal is simple: raid the long-lost treasure vault beneath **Raijinsho Island**, now home to the feared **White Dragon Harann Nemmonis**.

Yet fate weaves deeper threads.
A mysterious **Sea Urchin Newspaper Girl**, *Renata*, guides them not for gold, but for destiny. She is no mortal messenger, but a **Vaelithra Fragment**, a shard of the God of Time, here to preserve the sacred balance by freeing Raijinsho from Harann’s grasp before divine corruption consumes it forever.

The heroes must brave a voyage through lethal cold, mistrustful pirates, and a labyrinth of ice — to find not only treasure, but the **Koi Vaelithra**, a sacred fragment of time itself.

---

## DM’s Goals

Running *Into Raijinsho* means balancing **environmental dread** and **divine awe**:

1. **Build isolation.** The cold is as much an enemy as the dragon.
2. **Pace tension.** Use exploration and crew tension to let the fear breathe.
3. **Deliver wonder.** The encounter with the Koi Vaelithra should feel sacred, like touching eternity.

Let your players feel both *fragility and purpose.*

## Adventure Background

### The Sundering War and Vaeil’s Fractured Legacy

The world of **Dewanegara** still bears the scars of the **Sundering War** — the cosmic conflict that tore heaven and earth apart. It was a war not of armies, but of *belief itself*, where gods and demons bled through the seams of time to seize control of the mortal world.

When the war reached its climax, **Vaeil**, the **God of Time and Keeper of the Archives**, saw the threads of reality unraveling. Neither side would relent, and both were doomed to mutual extinction. To preserve existence, Vaeil broke the laws of his own domain — rewriting history.

He shaped an outcome where the gods won… but at the cost of the world’s balance. His interference was forbidden, and so, as punishment and penance, he shattered his essence into twelve divine shards known as the **Vaelithra**. Each fragment carried a sliver of his divine will and memory, scattered across worlds and time.

Some took the form of relics, some of spirits, and some — like the **Human Vaelithra, Renata** — became living avatars bound by purpose: to weave moments where fate might once again turn toward harmony.

---

### The Island of Raijinsho

Once, **Raijinsho Island** was not a wasteland of frost and death, but a place of quiet devotion. Its people lived in harmony with the storms that circled their shores, worshipping the **Serpent God of Health and Envy** — a patron of renewal through hardship.

Their faith taught that envy, when tempered, could drive the sick to seek healing and the weak to seek strength. Shrines carved into the cliffs once shimmered with offerings of jade, coral, and carved fangs representing rebirth through struggle.

Among these denizens thrived a notorious yet noble band of pirates known as the **Sacred Eye**. Though branded criminals by Thundrum City’s rulers, they were protectors of the seas, shielding travelers and local traders from slavers and monsters alike. The Sacred Eye built their stronghold within the caverns of Raijinsho — half sanctuary, half treasure vault.

When the **Sundering’s aftermath** twisted magic and weather across Dewanegara, the Sacred Eye vanished, leaving their treasures buried beneath the island’s waves and their oaths buried in history.

---

### Harann Nemmonis — The Frost Sovereign

In the void of that disappearance came **Harann Nemmonis**, a cruel **Adult White Dragon** drawn by the whisper of lingering magic and the promise of dominion. She claimed Raijinsho as her eternal lair, her will manifesting as an endless, supernatural winter that choked all warmth from sea and sky.

Her cruelty is not born of madness but *entitlement* — she views herself as a god of the new age, the rightful successor to the Serpent whose temples she desecrated. Her breath froze the shrines, her claws tore through the vaults, and her curse bound the dead to her service.

Those who tried to reclaim the Sacred Eye’s treasure — pirates, mercenaries, even knights — fell to her blizzard. Their corpses became **Frost-Cursed Mariners**, servants bound by her magic to guard her hoard for eternity.

Raijinsho became a graveyard of greed.

---

### The Koi Vaelithra — Fragment of Warm Eternity

Hidden deep beneath the frozen caverns lies one of Vaeil’s divine shards: the **Koi Vaelithra**. This fragment manifests as a silver-scaled koi spirit that swims within a magically warm subterranean pond — untouched by Harann’s frost.

The Koi’s power preserves a small circle of serenity, a final remnant of the Serpent God’s domain. Its divine energy continually resists Harann’s control, and if fully awakened, could *restore the island’s natural balance* — but the dragon’s corruption grows stronger each year.

The **Human Vaelithra, Renata**, foresaw this decay through the Archives of Time. To preserve the fragment and Raijinsho’s sanctity, she *stepped backward through time itself*, appearing in the current age to orchestrate one decisive event: the removal of Harann Nemmonis.

By weaving mortal greed, divine design, and the illusion of coincidence, Renata ensures that a band of capable adventurers will board Captain Thania’s ship at the right moment — unknowingly answering a god’s summons hidden within a pirate’s bargain.

---

### The Divine Threads of Purpose

This is not a story of divine prophecy fulfilled, but of mortals entangled in one.
Renata’s motives are not kindness — they are necessity. The Vaelithra cannot act directly without collapsing time’s flow, so she sets mortals into motion, *sacrificing their comfort for continuity*.

Those who follow her will walk the thin edge between survival and sanctity.
Those who defy her will still play their part.
For in Vaeil’s grand design, **fate is not a promise — it’s a pattern.**

---

## Adventure Outline

The adventure begins not with destiny declared, but with **a rumor sold for gold**—and a divine thread hiding beneath it.

**Captain Thania Schmidt**, a ruthless yet pragmatic pirate, seeks to reclaim the legendary treasure of the **Sacred Eye**, a mythic crew once said to have buried their greatest wealth beneath **Raijinsho Island**. Her ambitions are far from noble—she only desires the gold, gems, and spoils the dragon guards.

But this particular raid is not chance. It is the quiet design of **Renata**, the **Human Vaelithra**, who disguised herself as a humble newspaper girl to ensure the right heroes take Thania’s bait. Her true purpose is divine: to restore Raijinsho to its living color, and to protect the **Koi Vaelithra** from being forever entombed under Harann’s frost.

Unbeknownst to Thania or her crew, the voyage itself fulfills a **temporal ripple** — one Renata planted long ago when she traveled back through the Archives to undo the future where Raijinsho remains dead and silent.

---

### The Sacred Eye’s Echo

For centuries, the legend of the Sacred Eye persisted among pirates of **Thundrum City**, whispered in taverns thick with salt and deceit. The story tells of a final voyage, where the crew hid their treasure beneath the Serpent God’s temple on Raijinsho.

When the island froze under Harann’s dominion, countless captains sought the gold, but none returned. Their names faded into ice-bound ghosts—the first of the **Frost-Cursed Mariners**.

Thania’s **first mate, Kaelen**, lost a brother in one of those doomed expeditions. It’s why Thania waited—gathering information, salvaging charts, and stealing old logbooks until she was certain she knew where the dragon slept.

Now, with desperation biting harder than the cold, she has decided it’s time to strike—so long as she finds others foolish or strong enough to serve as her vanguard.

---

### Renata’s Orchestration

Renata does not stumble upon the adventurers.
She finds them deliberately, standing upon the docks of **Thundrum Port** as the world drifts toward one of its repeating points of collapse.

Through her mortal guise, she plants curiosity, temptation, and the faint pulse of divine urgency in the hearts of the party. To her, they are not chosen *heroes*, but variables—souls that must move, or time itself will stagnate.

The players will see her as a quiet, strange girl clutching a bundle of newspapers. Only later will they realize her words shaped their path long before they set sail.

### Getting Started

The adventure begins on the **docks of Thundrum Port**, where the adventurers are resting, resupplying, or searching for new work. It’s **Sylvryn Day**, and the harbor thrums with life—hawkers shouting, sailors brawling, the clang of chains and the hiss of steamships venting on the tide.

There’s no panic, no omen—just another day in a city that survives on salt and sin. Yet, for a reason none of them can name, the wind feels… expectant. The chill that brushes across their skin lingers longer than it should, as though the sea itself is holding its breath.

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The docks of **Thundrum Port** are loud and bright beneath the late morning sun. Fishmongers haggle, gulls scream overhead, and the sharp tang of brine and ale fills the air. Amidst the crowd, a calm voice cuts through the noise—
>
> “News from the northern seas!”
>
> A **young newspaper girl** stands at the edge of the pier, her coat patched and boots scuffed. Her eyes, a pale silver, shimmer faintly as she scans the crowd. Then she looks directly at you and smiles—as if she’s been waiting all along.

This quiet arrival replaces the expected chaos of Thundrum with something subtler: the feeling that fate has just turned its gaze.
Renata’s presence in this perfectly ordinary day marks the moment the adventurers’ story begins to unravel the stillness of time itself.

---

### Structure of Play

* **Act I – The Hook (Roleplay):** Renata delivers the message and draws the adventurers into Thania’s contract.
* **Act II – The Voyage (Tension & Survival):** The party endures the cold, growing distrust, and the first touch of the dragon’s influence.
* **Act III – The Ice (Exploration & Combat):** The final confrontation and the discovery of the Koi Vaelithra.
* **Act IV – The Thaw (Resolution):** The dragon’s defeat lifts the curse, setting the stage for the next fragment.

# Chapter 1 - Hook At Thundrum Port

## The Docks of Fate

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The docks of **Thundrum Port** are alive with chaos and commerce. Dockhands shout, gulls scream, and the air smells of brine, sweat, and spilled ale. A ship’s bell rings in the distance as sailors haul crates of spice and contraband beneath the morning sun.
>
> The day feels ordinary—too ordinary. Even the chill that lingers on the wind seems only the work of the season, not the warning of something greater. Yet beneath the noise, the rhythm of the harbor feels… waiting.

This chapter introduces the players to **Renata** (the Human Vaelithra) and **Captain Thania Schmidt**, setting the hook for their voyage to Raijinsho.

---

## A Subtle Intrusion

As the players navigate the port—whether restocking, seeking work, or boasting of past victories—one voice cuts through the din.

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> “News from the northern seas!”
>
> The cry is clear, carried not by desperation but precision. A small **newspaper girl** stands at the end of the pier, a stack of papers under one arm. Her clothes are plain, her boots worn from salt, yet her silver-gray eyes gleam like still water catching sunlight.
>
> She studies your group for only a moment before approaching with unnerving certainty.

---

## The Unlikely Messenger

Renata wastes no time. Her tone is polite but focused, as though reciting something that has already happened.

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> “Captain Thania Schmidt of the *Glowing Gate* seeks a capable crew for a northern expedition—*Raijinsho Island.*
> She pays in gold and allows half share of any *magic relics* found on-site. Departure: next high tide.”
>
> She pauses, her gaze sharpening. “She’s not hiring dreamers. You look… real enough.”

### DM Information

* **Identity:** Renata is the **Human Vaelithra**, a living shard of Vaeil, manifesting in mortal guise.
* **Stat Base:** *Scout* (MM p. 349), modified with **Persuasion +5**, **Insight +4**, and the ability to cast *Guidance* once per short rest.
* **Goal:** Ensure the party accepts Thania’s contract and boards before the next tide.
* **Atmosphere Cue:** The nearby gulls quiet as she speaks. The air feels still, as if the sea is holding its breath.

A successful **Insight (DC 18)** reveals something timeless behind her calm demeanor—an echo of memory far older than her years. Reward recognition with **Inspiration**.

---

## A Moment to Prepare (Act 0)

Renata points toward a ship moored a few piers away—a battered caravel with patched sails and a painted name barely legible: *The Glowing Gate.*

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> “She’s waiting. If you’re interested, tell her Renata sent you.”
>
> The girl’s silver eyes linger for a heartbeat longer, then she slips into the crowd. One blink—and she’s gone, as if she’d never been there.

Players may use this moment to roleplay freely—decide whether to trust the offer, question locals, or make lore checks to recall Raijinsho’s history or the Sacred Eye pirates (use your original DC table here).

If they ask around, common folk dismiss the rumor:

> “Raijinsho? Nobody sails there now. Ice took it years ago.”
>
> “Captain Schmidt? She’s mad—but she pays well if you live long enough to collect.”

---

## The Pirate’s Terms

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The *Glowing Gate* lists lazily against the pier, her hull patched and her figurehead weathered beyond recognition. A short, powerfully built woman waits at the gangplank—sun-browned, scarred, and unimpressed.
>
> “You the ones Renata sent?” she says without greeting. “Good. Saves me shouting.”

### DM Information

**Captain Thania Schmidt** uses *Bandit Captain* stats with **Wisdom (Insight) +4**.
She’s pragmatic, mercenary-minded, and deeply superstitious, though she hides it under cynicism.
She doesn’t trust magic, and sees the adventurers as expendable muscle.

#### Dialogue Highlights

* “It’s simple. You help me clear the island of that overgrown lizard and her frost-ridden dead, we split the spoils.”
* “You take half the *magic stuff*—scrolls, crystals, whatever nonsense you fancy. I take coin, gems, and trade goods.”
* “You want gold? Prove you’ll live to spend it.”

#### Negotiation

* **Persuasion DC 17:** Thania grudgingly adds a **5,000 gp guaranteed payout** upon return.
* **Failure:** She grows cold and dismissive, but Renata’s later intervention (see below) ensures the voyage still proceeds.

---

## Vaelithra Intervention (Fail-Safe)

If negotiations collapse or the party refuses:

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The world freezes—not metaphorically, but truly. The breeze stops mid-whisper, gulls hang suspended in the air, and even the surf halts in mid-splash.
>
> Renata stands again at the dock’s edge, voice calm but undeniable:
> “Five thousand gold upon return. That will satisfy you both.”
>
> Time resumes, leaving only the faint scent of ozone. Thania blinks, shudders, and nods as though it were her own idea.

Grant **Advantage** on the next **Persuasion** roll; Thania reluctantly agrees.

---

## Departure Timeline

Once the deal is struck:

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> “Good,” Thania says curtly. “We sail before the tide turns. Pack what you need, say your prayers, and don’t be late. Raijinsho won’t wait for anyone.”

The ship prepares swiftly. Dockhands load the last crates, Kaelen shouts orders from the rigging, and the city noise fades beneath the growing roar of the sea.

When the *Glowing Gate* finally casts off, the light of **Sylvryn Day** glints across the waves like fractured glass—bright, beautiful, and fleeting.

# Chapter 2 - The Journey and The Cold

## Setting Sail

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The **Glowing Gate** groans away from **Thundrum Port**, its weathered hull cutting into the turbulent waves. The noise of the city fades fast, replaced by the creak of timber and the snap of patched sails. The air, though still early in the day, has a brittle, chilling edge—the first, subtle touch of the dragon’s dominion. **Captain Thania Schmidt** gives you a single, grim nod. “Forty-eight hours to Raijinsho,” she says, her voice hard. “If the cold doesn’t kill you, my crew might.”

The voyage to **Raijinsho Island** is a two-day (48-hour) test of endurance. This chapter is designed to:

1.  **Drain Resources:** Challenge the party's ability to stay warm and alert.
2.  **Build Tension:** Establish the mistrust between the adventurers and the pirate crew.
3.  **Foreshadow the Dragon:** Introduce the unnatural cold and Harann's icy minions.

---

### Environmental Rules: The Chill of Nemmonis

As the ship nears the **Adult White Dragon's (Harann Nemmonis')** domain, the cold is intensified by her ancient, corrupt magic.

The environment progresses in danger over the 48-hour period. Use or adapt the following:

| Phase | Hours Elapsed | Condition | Effects (DMG p. 110 Adaptation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Biting Cold** | 0–12 h | A relentless wind from the north. | Creatures without **cold weather gear** suffer **Disadvantage** on **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** and **Wisdom (Perception)** checks. |
| **2. Severe Cold** | 12–24 h | Dense, chilling mist. Breath fogs instantly. | Each hour, a creature not protected must make a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw** or gain one level of **Exhaustion**. |
| **3. Extreme Cold** | 24–48 h | Sleet, frost, and near-freezing sea spray. | The **Constitution saving throw DC rises to 12**. Failure results in gaining one level of **Exhaustion** AND taking **2d6 Cold damage**. |

Encourage players to ration firewood, spell slots, and food. Warmth becomes a crucial resource.

---

### Voyage Random Encounter and Omen Table

Roll 1d6 every 6–8 hours, or when the party rests, to inject tension and flavor.

| d6 | Event | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Ghost Fog** | Visibility drops to 15 feet. Figures seem to move within the mist, but they vanish when approached. A creature making a successful **Wisdom (Perception) DC 14** check sees a faint, stylized **Koi symbol** briefly flicker in the gloom. |
| 2 | **Frost Bloom** | A sudden, unnatural rime flowers across metal and cloth. Anyone using a metallic weapon or wearing heavy armor must scrape the frost off (costing 10 minutes) or suffer **Disadvantage** on their next **Attack Roll** due to brittle ice. |
| 3 | **Crew Argument** | **Kaelen**, the First Mate, violently accuses a deckhand of hearing the "ice whisper." A successful **Charisma (Persuasion) DC 15** check can calm the crew; failure leads to lasting **Distrust** from the pirates towards the party. |
| 4 | **Renata’s Dream** | During a long rest, one PC has a vivid dream of a **silver-scaled Koi** swimming through black ice toward a source of deep, subterranean warmth. The PC wakes with a sense of quiet purpose and gains **Advantage** on their next saving throw against cold. |
| 5 | **Drifting Wreck** | The *Glowing Gate* passes the remains of a doomed expedition. An active search (**Intelligence (Investigation) DC 14**) of the wreckage uncovers frozen corpses branded with the **Sacred Eye** symbol, confirming the fate of past raiders and foreshadowing the Frost-Cursed. |
| 6 | **Silent Strike** | A sudden bolt of solid ice, a **frozen lightning** strike, crashes into the sea, rocking the ship and showering the deck with shards. All on deck must succeed on a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or take **1d6 piercing damage**. |

---

### Roleplay Thread: Kaelen’s Grudge

During the second day, when the cold has fully set in, **Kaelen** (Bandit stats) will approach the party. Her tension comes from a deep, personal source—the death of her brother.

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> Kaelen leans on the frozen railing, her breath smoking in the wind. She doesn’t look at you. “My brother went after that island five years ago. He thought the **Sacred Eye’s** gold was waiting for him. Harann sent back his body, frozen solid to a piece of driftwood.”
>
> Her jaw tightens. “So forgive me if I don’t cheer for you high-and-mighty heroes chasing ghosts and magic. I hope you’re better fighters than he was, or we’ll all end up Frost-Cursed.”

**Insight DC 13** reveals that her contempt is a mask for her grief and fear; she is intensely loyal to Captain Thania. A successful **Charisma (Persuasion) DC 15** check, or a genuinely empathetic response, will soften her tone.

**Success:** Kaelen grudgingly respects the party's mettle. She shares a final, critical piece of lore: **“The Dragon-Kin Soldiers carry horns. They use them to shift the ice and block tunnels. If you hear the horn, change your plan.”**

**Failure:** Kaelen remains suspicious and distrustful, making it harder to secure supplies or help from the pirate crew later on.

### A Silence on the Waves

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The second night bleeds into gray morning. The fog thickens until the horizon vanishes, and the *Glowing Gate* feels like it’s sailing through milk. The air grows so still that even the sails sag.
>
> Then, faintly, the sound of bells — soft, rhythmic, impossibly close — rings through the mist.

A **DC 15 Perception check** reveals ghostly shapes drifting on the water ahead: another ship, iced over and partially submerged.
If approached, the deck bears the sigil of the **Sacred Eye** carved into frozen wood.

---

### Encounter: *The Frost-Cursed Mariners*

**Setup:**
Four to six undead sailors (use *Wight* or *Ghoul* stats, tinted by frost). They are remnants of Thania’s predecessors — pirates who sought the Sacred Eye treasure decades ago. Their eyes glow faint blue, their flesh stiff as glass.

>  **Read-Aloud Text (Combat Intro):**
> The shapes on the deck move — slow, deliberate. Ice cracks as they turn toward you.
>
> The first raises its head. Frost falls from its beard in sheets.
>
> “*You came for gold...*” it rasps. “*So did we.*”

---

#### Combat Notes

* **Environment:** Slippery frozen deck (Dex Save DC 12 or fall prone if dashing).
* **Tactics:** They drag intruders into the freezing water (Grapple DC 13 → Con Save DC 14 or take 2d6 cold dmg each turn).
* **Twist:** On the third round, Kaelen recognizes one of them — her brother.

---

### Kaelen’s Moment

If Kaelen is present, she freezes mid-fight.

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> Her sword lowers. Her lips part but no sound comes out.
>
> “...Brother?”
>
> The creature twitches, then lunges — not out of hate, but reflex. Its eyes flicker with a moment of pain before the frost claims it again.

**Player Choice Moment:**

* A **Persuasion DC 17** or **Religion DC 15** can help Kaelen strike the final blow with peace.
* Failure causes her to hesitate, leaving her open to attack (auto-hit for 2d6 cold).
  When the undead falls, the ice around it melts slightly, revealing a **rusted locket** engraved with the Serpent symbol — foreshadowing the Koi Vaelithra’s power.

---

### Aftermath

The *Glowing Gate* drifts in silence. The fog seems heavier after the fight, pressing close like a held breath.

Thania orders the remains burned with black powder, her voice flat.
Kaelen says nothing, only stares at the melting ice where her brother fell.

If the players comfort her or help with the ritual, she will later defend them fiercely against Thania’s doubts — a trust that can change the tone of Chapter 3 entirely.

---

### Reward

* **500 XP each** (for mercy, combat, or giving peace).
* The **Locket of the Serpent Eye**:

  * Once per long rest, as a bonus action, the bearer may gain **Resistance to Cold Damage for 1 minute**.
  * When used, it glows faintly — a pulse of the Vaelithra’s warmth.

---

### Transition: The Ice Horizon

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> Hours later, the mist parts just enough to reveal the impossible — a stretch of solid ice spanning the ocean ahead. Beyond it, jagged peaks pierce the clouds. Lightning flickers through them in total silence.
>
> “Raijinsho,” Thania murmurs, gripping the rail.
>
> The ship slows, hull grinding against the first rim of frost.

The crew begins preparations to disembark, their breath already turning to mist.
Renata appears on deck for the first time since departure, her silver eyes faintly glowing in the dim light.
Without a word, she gazes toward the island, as though staring into her own reflection.

# Chapter 3 - Into The Labyrinth of Ice

This final chapter features the exploration of **Raijinsho Island**, the confrontation with **Harann Nemmonis**, and the discovery of the **Koi Vaelithra**.

## Phase 1: The Frozen Approach

The ship is forced to halt where the water becomes a vast, jagged expanse of **frozen sea**.

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The sea has become a nightmare. The water is no longer fluid, but a sheet of ancient, jagged ice that stretches for a mile, a vast, silent plain of danger. **Captain Thania** hands you a coil of rope and a grim look. “This is where we part ways, mercenaries. Good luck. We’ll be waiting for your signal.”

### The Treacherous Sea (Skill Challenge)

The half-mile journey across the frozen sea maintains the **Extreme Cold** rules (DC 12 Con save/hour, 2d6 Cold damage on failure). To safely and quickly reach the island shore, the party must collectively achieve **6 successes** before accumulating 3 failures.

| Available Checks (DC 15) | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wisdom (Survival)** | To find stable paths and navigate by the shifting winds. |
| **Strength (Athletics)** | To climb over high, slippery ice ridges and secure ropes. |
| **Intelligence (Nature)** | To predict thin ice or weak points in the sea. |
| **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** | To traverse slick, uneven patches without falling. |

**Consequence of 3 Failures:** The party falls into a hidden crevasse or triggers a patch of thin ice. They take **2d6 bludgeoning damage** from the fall and must spend one round stabilizing and climbing out (using a DC 13 Athletics check).

#### Ambush: The Corrupted Patrol

Midway across the ice, the party is attacked.

* **Enemies:** **Three Frost-Cursed Mariner** and **One Frost-Cursed Captain**.
* **Dragon's Foreshadowing:** During the combat, a shadow should pass briefly overhead—a fleeting sign that **Harann Nemmonis** is aware of the intruders, though she is too arrogant to intervene personally at this distance.

#### Frozen Corpse Loot (Reinforce Survival)

Allow players to search one or two frozen corpses they pass along the way (use the loot table from the *Story Outline.md* file). The most useful find would be the **Scroll of *Protection from Energy*** (Cold only) or the **Potent Antifreeze** to help with the Extreme Cold saves.

#### The Snow-Covered Shore

The party reaches a wide beach buried under deep snow, which counts as **difficult terrain**. Guarding the central entrance to the **Cave Labyrinth** are the dragon's organized forces.

* **Enemies:** **Two Frost-Cursed Kobold** and **Two Frost-Cursed Soldier** (use *Ogre* or *Berserker* stats with cold resistance).
* **Tactics:** The soldiers utilize **Hit-and-Run** tactics, harassing the party and retreating into the caves if severely threatened, ready to ambush again inside.

## Phase 2: The Cave Labyrinth

The party has successfully crossed the **Treacherous Sea** and navigated the initial snow-covered shore. They stand at the mouth of the ice-cave labyrinth.

### Encounter 1: The Serpent's Heart Lock (Puzzle/Bypass)

This encounter happens immediately upon entering the cave.

The first tunnel segment is a short, slick path leading to a choice: an immediate, narrow path to the right, or the main, wide tunnel straight ahead.

#### The Koi Vaelithra Lock (The Right Path)

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The narrow tunnel to your right terminates quickly at a solid door of **obsidian and bronze**, unlike the ice and rock everywhere else. Carved into its surface is an ancient **coiled serpent**, the symbol of the **Serpent God of Health and Envy**. There are no handles, only shallow indentations on the serpent's scales, and the air here is faintly, unnaturally warm. This path is clearly sealed and untouched by the dragon's presence.

* **The Problem:** This door leads to the **trap-free way** to the **Koi Vaelithra** in the **Warm Pond**, but it is sealed by the ancient denizen's **Geothermal Lock**.
* **The Key:** The puzzle requires diverting the cave's natural geothermal energy.
* **Solution (DC 15 Intelligence Checks):**
    * A successful **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)** check reveals three specific scales on the carving that act as pressure points, and a sequence of lights/glyph patterns around the serpent's eyes.
    * A successful **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)** check determines the correct **lever sequence** needed to divert the geothermal energy from a hidden source, triggering the door.
* **Success:** The bronze door *hisses* open, revealing a short, clear, **trap-free tunnel** leading directly to the **Warm Pond** where the **Koi Vaelithra** waits. *(Note: Players should be encouraged to complete the main objective first, but this is a clear-cut victory path if they choose it.)*
* **Failure:** A failed check results in a temporary jet of harmless steam. The party must wait **ten minutes** before attempting the check again.

### Encounter 2: The Pirate’s Trap (The Labyrinthine Path)

The party continues down the main, wider tunnel straight ahead, which appears to be the most heavily used path.

#### The Frozen Treasure Repository (Wide Area / Hexagonal Room)

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The main passage widens here, and a tunnel to the left leads into a **hexagonal chamber**. This space was clearly once a storage room for the **Sacred Eye** pirates. The walls are lined with empty, broken weapon racks and scattered debris, all covered in a thick layer of frost. In the center, a huge, black **ship’s chest** is completely encased in a shimmering dome of ancient, **magically-reinforced ice**.

* **Risk/Reward:** The **Frozen Chest** inside contains the **small hoard of treasures** (500 gp and two *Potions of Greater Healing*).
* **The Trap Trigger:** The hexagonal room is a tripwire. **Harann’s minions** are not stupid; they know raiders always go for the loot first. As soon as the party begins to *attempt to free the chest* (takes 10 minutes of effort) or enters the **tunnel directly in front of the hexagonal room**, the ambush is sprung.

#### The Dragon-Kin Ambush (Tunnel Beyond the Hexagonal Room)

This combat encounter should occur in the **tunnel area in front of the hexagonal room** (or *after* it, as you described). This makes retreat difficult and focuses the fight in a choke point.

* **Enemies:** **Two Frost-Cursed Kobold** (to harass/distract), **One Frost-Cursed Ogre**, and **One Frost-Cursed Chieftain**.
* **Tactics:** The Chieftain orders the Ogre to hold the tunnel entrance while the Kobolds use ranged attacks or try to flank. The Chieftain focuses on using the environment.
* **The Hazard:** The Dragon-Kin know this tunnel is rigged. The Chieftain can use a **Minor Lair Action** (once per round, on its initiative count) to trigger a partial **ceiling collapse** of ice (DC 14 Dexterity Save or take 3d6 bludgeoning damage and be restrained by ice shards).
* **Loot & Lore:** The defeated *Frost-Cursed Chieftain* drops the **Dragon-Kin Warhorn**. The horn is cracked, but a successful **DC 15 Charisma (Performance)** check allows it to be used once to cause a **minor cave-in** or to scare off minor minions, confirming Kaelen's warning.

## Phase 3: The Climax

After defeating the ambush and passing through the tunnels, the path leads directly up to the final area.

### The Lair Entrance

This final segment is the last stretch of the main tunnel leading up to the large circular chamber. The **Extreme Cold** rules should be at their most punishing here.

### The Final Confrontation: Harann Nemmonis

The party enters the large, circular top chamber—the main lair of **Harann Nemmonis** (Adult White Dragon, CR 13). The confrontation proceeds as planned.

## Phase 4: The True Objective (The Koi Vaelithra)

After the dragon is defeated or fled, the party can now safely use the **Geothermal Lock** and access the secret path.

* **The Warm Pool:** The door from the first chamber leads to the warm, geothermally fed chamber.
* **The Silver Scale:** The **Koi Vaelithra** floats in the water, leaving behind the **Silver Scale** (the fragment) for the players, granting **Resistance to Cold Damage** for 24 hours (the divine boon).

This revised flow honors the specificity of your map, ensuring a smooth and logical progression through the lair. Let me know if you would like me to detail the **Epilogue** next!

### The Divine Boon and Fail-Safe

* **Boon:** The Koi emits a subtle, powerful energy. Any character who touches the water or the **Silver Scale** (see below) gains **Resistance to Cold Damage** for the next 24 hours.
* **The Silver Scale:** The party finds a single **Silver Scale** left on the edge of the pool. This is the **Koi Vaelithra** fragment itself.
* **DM Fail-Safe (If TPK is imminent):** If the party is wiped out or in dire need of help during the fight with Harann, the **Koi Vaelithra** freezes time. It appears before the players, restoring their resources to a **Long Rest** state while their enemies remain frozen. The Koi then whispers its cryptic message about the next fragment and leaves the **Silver Scale** as a sign before time resumes. This maintains narrative continuity despite a near-defeat.

**Resolution:**

* **The Payout:** Captain Thania Schmidt keeps her word. The party receives the **5,000 gp guaranteed payout**. Any **Magic Items** the party recovered (including the **Silver Scale** of the Koi Vaelithra) are their personal spoils.

## The Guiding Fragment’s Final Word (The Critical Interaction)

The scene is set for a brief, final conversation. **Renata** finds the party *before* they board the ship, away from the pirates.

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> You are walking back to the shore when the urchin girl, **Renata**, steps out from behind a newly thawed stack of cargo. Her clothes are ragged, but her eyes—those unnervingly silver eyes—shine with a deep, powerful relief you’ve never seen on a child. She offers a small, knowing smile, clutching her worn leather notebook.

**Renata:** “You have the shard now, yes? Good. You must hold on to it. Not for the gold, but for the life it can bring back to this world. That makes it... **Two of Twe—**”

She cuts off instantly, a flicker of panic crossing her face before she forces a quick, sharp laugh that doesn't quite reach her eyes.

**Renata:** “—I mean, **Two of Eleven**. Forgive me. Sometimes I lose count.”

An unsettling stillness follows the correction. The players have a chance to act here.

#### Player Interaction: Pressing the Guide

Any character who makes a **DC 16 Insight check** realizes the panic was genuine and that the number **Twelve** was the truth, but the second number, **Eleven**, was a conscious, protective lie.

If the players choose to confront her, they can use their social skills:

| Action                  | Skill Check (DC 16)         | Outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :---------------------- | :-------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gentle Inquiry**      | **Charisma (Persuasion)**   | **Success:** She briefly lowers her guard. “They made me forget, but they can’t make me unknow. There are **twelve** pieces. And I am **one** of them. Tell no one.” She then delivers the hint (see below) and disappears. |
| **Forceful Demand**     | **Charisma (Intimidation)** | **Success:** She flinches but stands her ground. "I am only a messenger, and I am telling you only what you *need* to know." She then delivers the hint (see below) and is immediately gone, fleeing in distress.           |
| **Failure (Any Check)** | —                           | She maintains her mask. “It’s just a game I play! I told you, I lose count.” She quickly delivers the hint and fades into the breeze before they can press her again.                                                       |

Regardless of the outcome, she concludes with the hook for the next adventure:

**Renata:** “The next fragment is not ice and cold. Look for the **Mask of the Harlequin**. It is worn by a Warlock’s phantom, hidden inside the **Violet Carnival’s Demiplane**—a place of false mirrors and fleeting joy.”

She gives a final, silent nod and fades into the crisp ocean breeze, leaving the party with more gold, a piece of a shattered god, and the shocking realization that their innocent guide is, herself, one of the divine fragments. The sails of the *Glowing Gate* rise, and the journey continues.

# Chapter 1: The Voyage to Raijinsho

## Setting the Scene

*The Glowing Gate* groans as her sails catch the evening wind. The sea beyond Thundrum Port stretches endlessly, a dark mirror under silver moonlight. Lanterns sway with each roll of the deck, casting shadows across scarred sailors who mutter silent prayers.

Captain **Thania Schmidt** stands at the helm, her gaze fixed on the horizon. Her crew obeys her every barked command, but the name Raijinsho has already hollowed their courage. Even the waves seem colder as if warning of the island’s curse. Among them, a young man named **Rorin**, sang a shanty about lost love and frozen shores, while the ship’s cook, **Silas**, a half-orc with a warm heart, silently prepared more food, knowing they would need it for the long, cold journey.

### Key Scenes on the Voyage

#### First Night at Sea (Roleplay Opportunity)

The adventurers are invited to dine with **Captain Thania** and her first mate below deck. The air is thick with salt, woodsmoke, and stew—but conversation drifts inevitably to the journey’s purpose.

**Roleplay Hooks:**

* **Thania** shares how **Harann Nemmonis** slaughtered half her old crew. She hides her thirst for revenge beneath bravado.
* A sailor, drunk on rum, whispers about **“Rime-bounds”**—sailors who vanish near **Raijinsho**, only to return as **icy husks**. He’s silenced quickly by others.
* If pressed, **Thania** admits the sea has been wrong lately: ships vanish without a trace, storms brew from clear skies, and even the stars seem swallowed by frost.

**Skill Checks:**

* **History** or **Arcana** (**DC 14**): Recall that dragons’ magic often corrupts not only their lairs, but the lands and seas around them.
* **Insight** (**DC 15**): Notice **Thania**’s bravado slipping when **Raijinsho** is mentioned—her fear is real, but so is her resolve.

#### A Frosty Challenge (The Ice Floe Trap)

In the middle of the second night, The Glowing Gate collided with something hard. The sound of metal scraping rang out against the ship's hull. A thick fog suddenly appeared and the temperature dropped drastically. A giant iceberg had appeared out of nowhere, stretching out in front of the ship's path.

**Skill Challenge:**
* **Athletics DC 14:** Breaking the ice chunks by force, risks damaging the ship.
* **Perception DC 15:** Navigate the ship through a narrow passage between ice floes, risking running aground.
* **Arcana DC 16:** Identify that this ice is not ordinary ice, but rather living ice, imbued with the ice magic of the dragon. If successful, players can use magic or heat attacks to weaken it significantly.
* **Persuasion DC 13:** Reassure the panicked crew to remain calm and follow orders, to avoid accidents.

#### Trouble on the Water (The Frost-Cursed Raiders)

On the third night, a storm crashes down. Waves claw at the deck, lightning cracks, and freezing rain stings like knives. From the black surf, figures claw aboard the ship—once human sailors, now warped by Harann’s breath.

Their skin glows with pale frost, eyes burning icy blue, barnacled weapons dripping seawater that freezes wherever it splashes. They hiss curses as they lunge:

“…All belong… to Harann…”

**Combat Encounter:**

* 1 **Frost-Cursed Captain** (reskinned Sahuagin Baron)
* 4–6 **Frost-Cursed Mariners** (reskinned Sahuagin or Bandits with cold damage)

**Special Traits:**

* Weapons deal +1d4 cold damage.
* On death, they shatter into shards of ice and vanish with the tide.
* The Captain can unleash a chilling roar (Recharge 5–6): creatures within 15 ft. must succeed a DC 13 Con save or be slowed until end of their next turn.

**Skill Challenge Alternative:**
Instead of direct combat, run a hybrid challenge:

* **Athletics DC 14:** Secure rigging.
* **Acrobatics DC 15:** Keep balance as the deck tilts violently.
* **Perception DC 16:** Spot raiders climbing over the rails.
* **Intimidation DC 13:** Rally panicked sailors to fight.

3 failures before 5 successes = The Glowing Gate is damaged, costing time and morale.

#### Vaelithra’s Quiet Moment

When the storm passes, and the raiders’ frozen remains are gone, calm settles uneasily. Below deck, the adventurers find the “newspaper girl”—Vaelithra in disguise. Her clothes are damp, yet she looks strangely unbothered, her gaze far beyond the walls of the ship.

**Dialogue Options:**

* “Storms pass. Time always finds a way to correct itself.”
* If pressed: “You’ll understand in Raijinsho. Just… don’t fear the impossible.”

**Insight DC 17:** Her words feel practiced, like she has spoken them across countless lifetimes.

## Arrival at Raijinsho

By dawn, jagged peaks rise from the horizon. The island of Raijinsho looms, cloaked in endless snow despite the summer season. Frost creeps across the ship’s rails as an unnatural chill gnaws at bone and spirit alike.

Captain Thania tightens her grip on the wheel, voice steady but heavy with dread:
“There she is—the island of death and fortune both. Ready yourselves… Harann Nemmonis awaits.”

The Glowing Gate anchors in a hidden cove—the only safe approach. Beyond the treeline, icy winds howl from the heart of the island, where the dragon’s lair lies waiting.

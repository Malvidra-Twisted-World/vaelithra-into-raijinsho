# Chapter 1: The Hook at Thundrum Port

## The Unlikely Messenger

The adventure begins with the party established at the docks.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The docks of **Thundrum Port** are a tempest of activity on this **Sylvryn Day**. The air is thick with the smells of drying salt, burning tar, and the sweat of laborers. Amidst the chaos of pirate and merchant vessels, a youthful, slightly built **sea urchin newspaper girl** makes her way directly toward you. Her clothes are common and slightly damp, and she clutches a small stack of papers under one arm. Her eyes are strikingly light—a cool, pale silver that seems to hold a wisdom far older than her apparent age—and she offers a concise, intense greeting.

### DM Information
The **Sea Urchin Newspaper Girl** is the **Human Vaelithra** named **Renata** fragment. She was specifically hired by **Captain Thania Schmidt** but took the job only to orchestrate this meeting. Use **Scout** statistics but replace Shortbow proficiency with proficiency in **Persuasion** and **Insight**, and the ability to cast *Guidance* once per short rest.

Her primary goal is to ensure the party accepts the contract and sets sail.

* **Dialogue Cue:** The Vaelithra is straightforward and professional about the job, but her enthusiasm for *this particular raid* is unnervingly high. If the party asks who pays her, she replies honestly, "Captain Thania Schmidt hired me to deliver a message to the strongest looking crew. I am just making sure I deliver it to the right people."
* **The Divine Pitch:** Her message is simple but emphasizes the cosmic stakes she knows are hidden: "Captain Schmidt offers **$50\%$ of the magic items** from the White Dragon's lair. But that dragon is a world-changer. If anyone is going to face it, the fate of many people—maybe even the **balance of the Sundering** itself—rests on it being people like you."
* **Subtle Influence:** If a player attempts an **Insight check (DC 18)**, they sense a strange, deep-seated urgency in her, as if the clock is ticking on a matter far grander than pirate gold. The DM may grant a point of **Inspiration** to any character who hesitates but decides to follow her lead, representing the Vaelithra's subtle push of fate.

### Development
If the party agrees to hear the full offer, the Vaelithra nods and points toward the **Glowing Gate** a few docks over, but pauses to allow the party a chance to discuss.

## The Moment to Prepare (Act 0)

The **Sea Urchin Newspaper Girl** has delivered her pitch and given the party directions to the *Glowing Gate*, but she pauses, leaving the final few feet of the journey to the players.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The sea urchin nods once, her strange silver eyes fixed on your group, and points toward a battered caravel, the **Glowing Gate**, a few docks down. "She's waiting. You should talk now before you approach." With that, she turns and darts back into the crowd, leaving you with the heavy knowledge of the dragon's challenge and the unsettling feeling that she knows far more than she let on. The time is yours to prepare before you commit to **Captain Thania Schmidt's** raid.

#### DM Information
This is the core roleplaying and bonding segment of the chapter. Encourage the players to discuss the extreme danger of facing an **Adult White Dragon (CR 13)** and the peculiar nature of the messenger. This is also the point where players can leverage their character's knowledge to uncover the deeper lore of **Dewanegara**.

Encourage players to make an Intelligence (History), Intelligence (Religion), or Wisdom (Survival) check to recall information about the world.

| Skill Check | DC | Information Revealed (Share only ONE of these per successful check) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intelligence (History)** | **DC 13** | The **Sundering** (35 A.S.) fractured the natural order of the world. Areas like **Raijinsho Island**, where the weather is magically fixed and unnatural, often become strongholds for powerful creatures, attracting greater dangers. |
| **Intelligence (History)** | **DC 18** | The Sundering was secretly ended by the God of Time, **Vaeil**, who manipulated the past. This act of rewriting the *Archives* made both the Gods and the Demons interested in controlling those fragments of time—the **Vaelithra**. |
| **Intelligence (Religion)** | **DC 15** | The god **Vaeil** shattered his soul into twelve **Vaelithra** fragments to prevent his work from being undone. To have encountered a Vaelithra means your path has intersected with a key event in the world's survival. |
| **Wisdom (Survival)** | **DC 16** | **White Dragons** are the most cruel and avaricious of the chromatics. Their magic makes their territory lethally cold, and the whispers around **Raijinsho** claim any injury sustained on the island tends to fester and heal slowly. |

#### Development
The players should determine their strategy: how to approach the negotiation, who will speak, and what they will demand. Once the players signal they have finished their discussion, they move on to the ship and the next scene.

That's fantastic! With the Introduction complete and the lore locked in, we can finalize **Chapter 1** as the critical "Act 0" transition before the journey.

## The Pirate's Terms

The **Sea Urchin Vaelithra** has done her part. Now the adventurers must commit to the contract with the pirate captain.

>[!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The **Glowing Gate** is a ship that has seen too much hard living. Its hull shows patched wood, and its rigging is frayed—a genuine pirate vessel, not a pleasure craft. Standing on the worn deck is **Captain Thania Schmidt**. She is a compact woman, dressed in rough leather and armed with two cutlasses belted across her waist. Her face is a map of scars and sun exposure, and her eyes, devoid of any empathy, are impatient. She nods curtly toward the dock, having seen the newspaper girl dart away, and gestures you aboard. "Don't waste my time. The offer is simple."

### DM Information
**Captain Thania Schmidt** (use **Bandit Captain** statistics, with a high **Wisdom (Insight)** modifier of +4) is a ruthless, desperate pragmatist. She knows the Adult White Dragon is an overwhelming threat, which is why she is willing to sacrifice the most valuable loot to hire the adventurers.

* **Dialogue Cue:** Thania is brief and focused only on the raid logistics and payment.
* **The Contract:** "It's a smash-and-grab on **Raijinsho Island**. My crew gets us to the safe drop-off point and secures the perimeter for the escape. Your job is the cave—**the Corrupted Ice Zombies, the Dragon-Kin Soldiers, the traps, and the final fight with Harann Nemmonis.**"
* **Payment Terms:** "The payment is **50% of any *magic items* or *unique artifacts* you pull from that central lair**. We take the bulk of the gold, gems, and common valuables. That's the deal. Say yes or no. I sail at the next high tide."
* **Negotiation:** Thania's terms are harsh, but fair for a pirate of her standing. Any **Charisma (Persuasion)** checks to increase the *magic item* share must beat her **Wisdom (Insight) DC 17**. If the party presses too hard for the gold share, she becomes hostile and might even draw a weapon, threatening to find a less greedy group.

That is a great point. Relying on a single "failure" point that ends the adventure can feel restrictive for players who try to push boundaries or use creative solutions. The goal is to allow their creativity while still ensuring the divine plot moves forward.

There is absolutely a way to prevent the "adventure ends here" outcome while honoring your lore. We just need to ensure the **Vaelithra's** influence is stronger than the party's rejection.

Here is a revised approach for the **Failure and Lore** section of **The Pirate's Terms**, which accommodates player creativity but gently steers them toward the contract:

### Failure and Lore (Revised)

If the party attempts to deceive **Captain Thania** (and fail the necessary checks) or if they try to reject the contract outright, the **Human Vaelithra** (Newspaper Girl) will intercede to ensure the mission goes forward.

* **Refusal:** If the party flatly refuses the mission, the Sea Urchin Vaelithra reappears instantly. She will not plead or beg. Instead, she will use her subtle divine influence and knowledge of time to change the circumstances.
    * **Vaelithra Intervention:** The Vaelithra looks directly at the most vocal character and, using the power of *Guidance*, suggests a single, crucial term that Thania might accept, such as guaranteeing that the party gets $50\%$ of the magic items **and** a guaranteed $5,000$ gold pieces payout upon return, regardless of the dragon's main gold pile.
    * She then quickly says, "She will agree to that. You must go," before vanishing again into the crowd.
    * Thania, startled by the Vaelithra's sudden return and demand, is momentarily unnerved. The DM should give the party **Advantage** on their next **Charisma (Persuasion)** check if they immediately counter with the Vaelithra's suggested term. Thania, realizing the messenger is more than she seems, will grudgingly accept the new term to rid herself of the unsettling girl.

### Departure Timeline

If the party agrees, Thania directs them to her skeptical first mate, Kaelen, for basic supplies and a final word: "Good. We sail in **less than an hour**. Get your gear and get ready to freeze. I won't wait for latecomers."

# Chapter 3: The Dragon’s Lair

## Setting the Scene

The cavern opens into a cathedral of ice. Towering stalactites glimmer like frozen spears, reflecting the pale blue glow of dragonfire frozen mid-breath. The ceiling vanishes into darkness, while beneath it sprawls **Harann Nemmonis**’s hoard—coins, weapons, shattered relics—all frozen together in glittering sheets.

The air is so cold it feels alive, biting at lungs and frosting armor. At the far end, curled like a storm waiting to break, lies Harann Nemmonis: an Adult White Dragon, her scales jagged like shards of glacial glass. Her eyes snap open as the adventurers enter, glowing like frozen suns.

> “Intruders… thieves… your bones will make fine ornaments for my halls. The treasure is mine alone.”

## Key Scenes

#### Entering the Hoard

The floor is slippery ice (**Acrobatics DC 13** each round of combat to avoid falling prone if moving more than half speed).

Chained to the wall: several frostbitten humanoids—remnants of failed treasure hunters. They are too weak to fight, but may beg for release mid-combat.

#### The Boss Fight: Harann Nemmonis

This should be a CR 13–15 challenge depending on party size (Adult White Dragon baseline, but with lair actions + regional effects emphasized).

**Lair Actions** (every round on initiative 20):

* **Blizzard Veil:** Icy wind fills the chamber. All non-dragons must succeed on a **DC 15 Con save** or be blinded until the end of their next turn.
* **Shattering Ice:** An icicle falls from the ceiling, targeting a random player (+10 to hit, **2d10 piercing + 2d10 cold**).
* **Frozen Shackles:** Frost creeps up from the floor, restraining one random creature (**DC 16 Str save** to break).

**Regional Effect** (active the moment they enter):

The temperature is so low that creatures without cold resistance take **2 cold damage** each round unless magically protected.

Spells or fire damage melt patches of ice, creating slick pools of water that refreeze at the start of the dragon’s next turn.

#### The Vaelithra Revealed

When the dragon drops below 50% HP, the pond at the back of the lair glows. The ice cracks, and from its waters emerges a silvery koi spirit—the Vaelithra of Raijinsho.

**Effect:** All adventurers regain the benefits of a long rest instantly.

> “The koi swims through the air like water, its scales shimmering with starlight. Time bends, and wounds mend as if battles never happened.”

If the adventurers are nearly defeated (TPK risk), the koi intervenes earlier, freezing the dragon in time for 1 round and restoring the adventurers to half HP + spell slots.

## Aftermath

As Harann Nemmonis shatters into ice and dust, silence falls over the cavern. For a heartbeat, it seems the island itself mourns her passing—then the koi Vaelithra glows brighter, radiating waves of warmth.

The frost gripping the cave walls begins to weep, rivulets of water running down to pool at the koi’s pond. Outside, the unnatural stormclouds dissolve, the air warms, and snow gives way to green. The adventurers step out of the dragon’s lair not into a frozen wasteland, but into a reborn Raijinsho: rocky cliffs blooming with moss, the cries of seabirds returning, and the smell of salt and life.

This island, once cursed in ice, now regains the beauty it once had.

The treasure hoard is vast, but much is fused into ice and impossible to remove. The koi Vaelithra offers one gift: a glowing fragment of Vaeil’s soul.

The disguised human Vaelithra (the newspaper girl) steps forward at this moment, smiling knowingly.

> “You’ve met her now. Another shard of the Keeper’s soul. Our paths will cross again, heroes. Time always finds its way.”

With that, the koi dissolves back into its pond, leaving behind a warm, eternal glow in the icy lair.

### Epilogue Hook

As the crew of the *Glowing Gate* celebrates, the disguised human Vaelithra (the “newspaper girl”) walks to the adventurers. With the island renewed behind her and the koi swimming lazily in the pond, she offers a soft smile:

> “The shard is safe again. One out of twelve, found by your hands. Eleven remain scattered across this world. When the time is right, we’ll meet again.”

She writes in her little notebook, closes it with a snap, and looks at the horizon.

The chapter closes as the sails of the *Glowing Gate* catch the warm sea breeze, carrying the heroes homeward—changed, and yet still at the beginning of a greater story.

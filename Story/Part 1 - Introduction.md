
# Introduction

## Running the Adventure

This adventure is intended for the **Dungeon Master (DM)** and assumes familiarity with the **Fifth Edition** core rules. The following section introduces the tone, world context, and framework for running ***Vaelithra – Into Raijinsho*** within the **Malvidra Twisted World** setting.

This module emphasizes **exploration and survival** rather than extended combat — each encounter is deliberate, deadly, and meant to be memorable. The focus lies on the island’s oppressive cold, the eerie stillness of a forgotten people, and the divine mystery lurking beneath the ice.

---

## Overview

***Vaelithra – Into Raijinsho*** is a high-level one-shot designed for **Level 7–9** characters, though it is best experienced at **Level 9** for full narrative and mechanical tension. It serves as both a standalone expedition and a key part of the ongoing **Vaelithra Saga**, where fragments of the shattered god **Vaeil** appear across time and space.

* **Author / Universe:** Malvidra Twisted World
* **World:** Dewanegara
* **Primary Themes:** *Exploration (Frozen Island), Survival (Extreme Cold), Divine Mystery*
* **Tone:** Harsh isolation, hidden divinity, and brief warmth in the dark
* **Timeline:** 6th Sol’thara, Sylvryn Day, Year 35 A.S. (After Sundering)

---

## Adventure Summary

The adventurers are drawn into the service of **Captain Thania Schmidt**, a hardened pirate chasing an old legend of the **Sacred Eye**—a brotherhood of thieves who once ruled the seas around Thundrum City. Her goal is simple: raid the long-lost treasure vault beneath **Raijinsho Island**, now home to the feared **White Dragon Harann Nemmonis**.

Yet fate weaves deeper threads.
A mysterious **Sea Urchin Newspaper Girl**, *Renata*, guides them not for gold, but for destiny. She is no mortal messenger, but a **Vaelithra Fragment**, a shard of the God of Time, here to preserve the sacred balance by freeing Raijinsho from Harann’s grasp before divine corruption consumes it forever.

The heroes must brave a voyage through lethal cold, mistrustful pirates, and a labyrinth of ice — to find not only treasure, but the **Koi Vaelithra**, a sacred fragment of time itself.

---

## DM’s Goals

Running *Into Raijinsho* means balancing **environmental dread** and **divine awe**:

1. **Build isolation.** The cold is as much an enemy as the dragon.
2. **Pace tension.** Use exploration and crew tension to let the fear breathe.
3. **Deliver wonder.** The encounter with the Koi Vaelithra should feel sacred, like touching eternity.

Let your players feel both *fragility and purpose.*

## Adventure Background

### The Sundering War and Vaeil’s Fractured Legacy

The world of **Dewanegara** still bears the scars of the **Sundering War** — the cosmic conflict that tore heaven and earth apart. It was a war not of armies, but of *belief itself*, where gods and demons bled through the seams of time to seize control of the mortal world.

When the war reached its climax, **Vaeil**, the **God of Time and Keeper of the Archives**, saw the threads of reality unraveling. Neither side would relent, and both were doomed to mutual extinction. To preserve existence, Vaeil broke the laws of his own domain — rewriting history.

He shaped an outcome where the gods won… but at the cost of the world’s balance. His interference was forbidden, and so, as punishment and penance, he shattered his essence into twelve divine shards known as the **Vaelithra**. Each fragment carried a sliver of his divine will and memory, scattered across worlds and time.

Some took the form of relics, some of spirits, and some — like the **Human Vaelithra, Renata** — became living avatars bound by purpose: to weave moments where fate might once again turn toward harmony.

---

### The Island of Raijinsho

Once, **Raijinsho Island** was not a wasteland of frost and death, but a place of quiet devotion. Its people lived in harmony with the storms that circled their shores, worshipping the **Serpent God of Health and Envy** — a patron of renewal through hardship.

Their faith taught that envy, when tempered, could drive the sick to seek healing and the weak to seek strength. Shrines carved into the cliffs once shimmered with offerings of jade, coral, and carved fangs representing rebirth through struggle.

Among these denizens thrived a notorious yet noble band of pirates known as the **Sacred Eye**. Though branded criminals by Thundrum City’s rulers, they were protectors of the seas, shielding travelers and local traders from slavers and monsters alike. The Sacred Eye built their stronghold within the caverns of Raijinsho — half sanctuary, half treasure vault.

When the **Sundering’s aftermath** twisted magic and weather across Dewanegara, the Sacred Eye vanished, leaving their treasures buried beneath the island’s waves and their oaths buried in history.

---

### Harann Nemmonis — The Frost Sovereign

In the void of that disappearance came **Harann Nemmonis**, a cruel **Adult White Dragon** drawn by the whisper of lingering magic and the promise of dominion. She claimed Raijinsho as her eternal lair, her will manifesting as an endless, supernatural winter that choked all warmth from sea and sky.

Her cruelty is not born of madness but *entitlement* — she views herself as a god of the new age, the rightful successor to the Serpent whose temples she desecrated. Her breath froze the shrines, her claws tore through the vaults, and her curse bound the dead to her service.

Those who tried to reclaim the Sacred Eye’s treasure — pirates, mercenaries, even knights — fell to her blizzard. Their corpses became **Frost-Cursed Mariners**, servants bound by her magic to guard her hoard for eternity.

Raijinsho became a graveyard of greed.

---

### The Koi Vaelithra — Fragment of Warm Eternity

Hidden deep beneath the frozen caverns lies one of Vaeil’s divine shards: the **Koi Vaelithra**. This fragment manifests as a silver-scaled koi spirit that swims within a magically warm subterranean pond — untouched by Harann’s frost.

The Koi’s power preserves a small circle of serenity, a final remnant of the Serpent God’s domain. Its divine energy continually resists Harann’s control, and if fully awakened, could *restore the island’s natural balance* — but the dragon’s corruption grows stronger each year.

The **Human Vaelithra, Renata**, foresaw this decay through the Archives of Time. To preserve the fragment and Raijinsho’s sanctity, she *stepped backward through time itself*, appearing in the current age to orchestrate one decisive event: the removal of Harann Nemmonis.

By weaving mortal greed, divine design, and the illusion of coincidence, Renata ensures that a band of capable adventurers will board Captain Thania’s ship at the right moment — unknowingly answering a god’s summons hidden within a pirate’s bargain.

---

### The Divine Threads of Purpose

This is not a story of divine prophecy fulfilled, but of mortals entangled in one.
Renata’s motives are not kindness — they are necessity. The Vaelithra cannot act directly without collapsing time’s flow, so she sets mortals into motion, *sacrificing their comfort for continuity*.

Those who follow her will walk the thin edge between survival and sanctity.
Those who defy her will still play their part.
For in Vaeil’s grand design, **fate is not a promise — it’s a pattern.**

## Adventure Outline

The adventure begins not with destiny declared, but with **a rumor sold for gold**—and a divine thread hiding beneath it.

**Captain Thania Schmidt**, a ruthless yet pragmatic pirate, seeks to reclaim the legendary treasure of the **Sacred Eye**, a mythic crew once said to have buried their greatest wealth beneath **Raijinsho Island**. Her ambitions are far from noble—she only desires the gold, gems, and spoils the dragon guards.

But this particular raid is not chance. It is the quiet design of **Renata**, the **Human Vaelithra**, who disguised herself as a humble newspaper girl to ensure the right heroes take Thania’s bait. Her true purpose is divine: to restore Raijinsho to its living color, and to protect the **Koi Vaelithra** from being forever entombed under Harann’s frost.

Unbeknownst to Thania or her crew, the voyage itself fulfills a **temporal ripple** — one Renata planted long ago when she traveled back through the Archives to undo the future where Raijinsho remains dead and silent.

---

### The Sacred Eye’s Echo

For centuries, the legend of the Sacred Eye persisted among pirates of **Thundrum City**, whispered in taverns thick with salt and deceit. The story tells of a final voyage, where the crew hid their treasure beneath the Serpent God’s temple on Raijinsho.

When the island froze under Harann’s dominion, countless captains sought the gold, but none returned. Their names faded into ice-bound ghosts—the first of the **Frost-Cursed Mariners**.

Thania’s **first mate, Kaelen**, lost a brother in one of those doomed expeditions. It’s why Thania waited—gathering information, salvaging charts, and stealing old logbooks until she was certain she knew where the dragon slept.

Now, with desperation biting harder than the cold, she has decided it’s time to strike—so long as she finds others foolish or strong enough to serve as her vanguard.

---

### Renata’s Orchestration

Renata does not stumble upon the adventurers.
She finds them deliberately, standing upon the docks of **Thundrum Port** as the world drifts toward one of its repeating points of collapse.

Through her mortal guise, she plants curiosity, temptation, and the faint pulse of divine urgency in the hearts of the party. To her, they are not chosen *heroes*, but variables—souls that must move, or time itself will stagnate.

The players will see her as a quiet, strange girl clutching a bundle of newspapers. Only later will they realize her words shaped their path long before they set sail.

| Chapter 1 | *The Hook at Thundrum Port* | Social interaction, roleplay, and negotiation. Introduction to Thania, Renata, and the pirate contract.                                   |

### Primary Objectives

* **Defeat or drive away Harann Nemmonis**, ending her dominion over Raijinsho Island.
* **Locate and secure the Koi Vaelithra**, ensuring its divine protection.
* **Survive the extreme cold** and maintain crew morale through the voyage.
* **Fulfill (or betray)** the pirate contract, shaping the group’s reputation across Thundrum and the seas beyond.

### Getting Started

The adventure begins on the **docks of Thundrum Port**, where the adventurers are resting, resupplying, or searching for new work. It’s **Sylvryn Day**, and the harbor thrums with life—hawkers shouting, sailors brawling, the clang of chains and the hiss of steamships venting on the tide.

There’s no panic, no omen—just another day in a city that survives on salt and sin. Yet, for a reason none of them can name, the wind feels… expectant. The chill that brushes across their skin lingers longer than it should, as though the sea itself is holding its breath.

>  **Read-Aloud Text:**
> The docks of **Thundrum Port** are loud and bright beneath the late morning sun. Fishmongers haggle, gulls scream overhead, and the sharp tang of brine and ale fills the air. Amidst the crowd, a calm voice cuts through the noise—
>
> “News from the northern seas!”
>
> A **young newspaper girl** stands at the edge of the pier, her coat patched and boots scuffed. Her eyes, a pale silver, shimmer faintly as she scans the crowd. Then she looks directly at you and smiles—as if she’s been waiting all along.

This quiet arrival replaces the expected chaos of Thundrum with something subtler: the feeling that fate has just turned its gaze.
Renata’s presence in this perfectly ordinary day marks the moment the adventurers’ story begins to unravel the stillness of time itself.

---

### Structure of Play

* **Act I – The Hook (Roleplay):** Renata delivers the message and draws the adventurers into Thania’s contract.
* **Act II – The Voyage (Tension & Survival):** The party endures the cold, growing distrust, and the first touch of the dragon’s influence.
* **Act III – The Ice (Exploration & Combat):** The final confrontation and the discovery of the Koi Vaelithra.
* **Act IV – The Thaw (Resolution):** The dragon’s defeat lifts the curse, setting the stage for the next fragment.
